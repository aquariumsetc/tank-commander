VOICE
Voice commands: "fire", "smoke", "medic", "supply", "map", "AP / HE / tracer / sabot", "full speed", "forward", "slow", "reverse", "stop", "lob", "direct". Needs microphone access; on iPhone it also needs an internet connection.
Open this same page on a second device (same account), tap COMPANION MAP there, and it mirrors the battlefield live while you play here.
View: You start in the gunner's sight — a first-person view down the barrel; the gold HULL marker shows which way the tracks point. Tap VIEW (or say "top down") for the overhead driver cam. Radar panel in the corner shows the whole battlefield (blue dots = team, red dots = detected enemies, houses = bases). Tap MAP to swap to the big tactical map (driver view goes to the corner).
Drive: The gun steers. Point the turret where you want to go and push the left stick up — the hull swings round to follow the gun. Further up = faster, down = reverse, release = brake. Push the stick left or right to strafe: the hull crabs sideways while the gun stays locked on target. When you're stopped, the hull stays put so you can aim freely. Tap LOCK to have the turret track the enemy you're looking at (leads moving targets; tap again to release).
Aim: In the gunner view, drag left/right on the right half to slew the turret. In top-down, tap where you want to point. Or tap TILT for gyro aim — tilt device left/right to swing turret, tilt back to raise the gun. The ⟲ button recalibrates.
Machine gun: Select MG and hold FIRE for continuous fire. It shreds scouts and light tanks from the side or rear but only sparks off heavy armour, and it overheats after about five seconds — watch the heat bar.
Elevation: Drag the ELEV slider above FIRE to lob shells over trees and rooftops — the gold ring shows where they land. Bottom = direct fire.
Keys: W/S gears, A/D strafe (gun stays on target), L lock target, mouse aims, click or space fires, ↑/↓ gun elevation (0 resets), 1-5 ammo (5 = machine gun), M map, C gunner/top-down, Q/E zoom, R supply, G medic, F smoke, N mute, V voice commands.
